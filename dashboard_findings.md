# Power BI Dashboard — Key Findings

## Overall No-Show Rate
- 28.52% of patients missed their scheduled appointments
- This means roughly 1 in 3 patients is not showing up

## No-Show Rate by Age Group
- Young Adults (18-35) miss the most appointments at 34.36%
- Children (0-17) are second highest at 32.27% — depends on parents
- Middle Age (36-60) are more responsible at 26.71%
- Seniors (60+) are the most reliable group at 20.46%
- Recommendation: Focus reminder efforts on younger patients

## No-Show Rate by Neighbourhood
- Gurigica has the highest no-show rate at 38.37%
- Jesus de Nazareth is second at 37.49%
- All top 9 neighbourhoods have no-show rates above 32%
- Recommendation: Target these neighbourhoods with stronger outreach

## No-Show Rate by Waiting Period
- Patients waiting more than 30 days miss 33.02% of appointments
- Patients with same day appointments miss only 21.35%
- Clear trend — the longer the wait, the higher the no-show rate
- Recommendation: Send follow-up reminders to patients waiting longer than 30 days
